Protocols with no token yet, ranked by what a token would be worth if the record stays as it is.

Implied value is this radar's own claim, from the sealed snapshot's TVL, 7-day growth and time listed — never a prediction that a token will be issued, or when. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry. Farm plans are a starting point, not financial advice: never pay a fee to claim, and never farm with money you cannot afford to lose.
